# Norm's Country Kitchen

This mod for Stardew Valley adds a food truck vendor who sells his homemade
regional delicacies in town every Tuesday. In real life, these are Pennsylvania
Dutch foods which I consider iconic, and which I grew up eating (or, in some
cases, avoiding), but in the mod it isn't specified and Norm never talks about
his home.

## Meet Norm

Norm is an ordinary, no-nonsense sort of guy who drives his truck from place to
place and sells his specialty foods. He isn't much of a talker, nor is he a
sentimental sort. He loves simple pleasures, like food, and likes to listen to
his radio while he works (if you have the optional Positional Audio mod
installed, you will be able to hear the radio while he's open).

## Requirements

* Stardew Valley 1.6.15+
* SMAPI 4.1.10+
* Content Patcher 2.6+
* Positional Audio 1.0+ (optional; used only to enable hearing Norm's music)

## Unlocking

Norm and his truck will become available automatically after you have played
for at least 10 days. On the next Monday, Lewis will send you a letter about
the truck. Starting from the day after that, Norm will come to town every
Tuesday, including for the Stardew Valley Fair on Fall 16.

On non-festival days, the truck is open for business from 8:00 am until 5:00
pm. At the Fair, his truck is available near Gus's barbecue, but he won't play
his radio. Unlike the barbecue, you still have to buy Norm's food.

## Making a Friend

Norm doesn't have any heart events or any other content of that sort, but he's
a real NPC and you can befriend him (doing so is not required for Perfection).
If you do, then instead of always going to the Saloon after he closes his shop,
he'll also visit some nice spots around Pelican Town (Town, Beach, and Forest)
to relax.

Norm's gift tastes:

* **Love**: Rhubarb Pie, Spaghetti, Rice Pudding, Pale Ale, Pickles, Birch
  Water (SVE)
* **Like**: Sugar, Wheat Flour, Oil, Birch Syrup (SVE), *his own products*

Norm's schedule:

* **Tuesday**: at the truck first thing in the morning. Mans the truck from
  8:00 until 5:00, then visits the saloon for a few hours before returning to
  the truck.
* **Tuesday (4+ hearts)**: at the truck first thing in the morning. Mans the
  truck from 8:00 until 5:00, then visits one of: the saloon, the town
  fountain, the beach's west pier, or the forest river.

After reaching 4 hearts, he'll ask you which is your favorite among Town,
Beach, and Forest. He'll add another spot in that location to his random
selection pool (so he'll go there more often):

* Town: the bridge leading to the library
* Forest: the east bank of the pond
* Beach: the shore by the bridge to the tide pools
